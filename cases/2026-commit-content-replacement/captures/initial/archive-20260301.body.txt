                <p class="text-justify"> Awadh Centre of Education established in 2009, is committed for 
                    sustained high quality education through its institutions. The primary aim of these 
                    educational institutions viz.  COMM-IT Career Academy and Institute of Vocational Studies, 
                    is to promote the educational interest of the lower strata of minority communities,
                    by providing them quality education for their overall growth and development.</p>
              
                  <p>
                      Our motto is holistic vision of education that never discounts the past , at the same 
                      time embraces the future with unwavering confidence in ability to shape it and harness 
                      its potentialities. The demand of knowledge world today is quality and specialization 
                      backed by rapid growth in the field of Science and Technology. This cannot be achieved 
                      unless is laid on imparting quality education at the grass root level. For achieving this aim , 
                      we need to intensively utilize the communication skills with the help of advance technologies 
                      in the field of education so that the teacher taught communication fulfills the qualitative 
                      mandates of the prescribed curriculum transactions.
